# ascess-1-ai — Hackathon Pitch Deck Presentation 📊

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Project Name**: ascess-1-ai
- **Tagline**: Enterprise AI-Powered Inclusive Accessibility & Web Audit Platform
- **Tech Stack**: Google Gemini 1.5 Pro, Supabase PostgreSQL, React 19, Express.js

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Massive Audience**: 1.3 Billion people globally need digital accessibility accommodations.
- **Inaccessible Web**: 96% of top 1 million website homepages have detectable WCAG violations.
- **Tool Fragmentation**: Developers & users juggle separate tools for contrast checking, screen reading, translation, and simplification.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — ascess-1-ai
- **Unified Accessibility Hub**: Ingests PDFs, images, URLs, and text into a single AI engine.
- **Gemini Copilot**: Context-aware document Q&A, 14-language formatting-preserving translation, and 6 text simplification modes.
- **Voice & Inclusive Suite**: Voice command navigation, dyslexic typography, reading rulers, and multi-format report exports.

---

## Slide 4: Architecture & Innovations
- **Low-Latency Gemini AI Pipeline**: Memory LRU caching & 1-step exponential retry handler.
- **Document Processing Engine**: `pdf-parse`, `cheerio` Web scraper, and Tesseract.js OCR.
- **Supabase Persistence**: Document metadata, user settings, audit reports, and history persistence.

---

## Slide 5: Future Scope & Impact
- Browser Extension for instant 1-click web page remediation.
- Real-time video captioning & automated audio description generator.
- Multi-tenant enterprise accessibility compliance dashboard.
