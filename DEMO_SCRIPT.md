# ascess-1-ai — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script 🎥

---

## 🎬 Act 1: Introduction & Problem (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Speaker**: "Hello everyone! Over 1.3 billion people globally live with disabilities or reading difficulties like dyslexia and visual impairment. Yet over 95% of web pages fail basic accessibility standards."
- **Screen**: Show `ascess-1-ai` Landing Page / Dashboard with dark glassmorphic UI.
- **Speaker**: "Introducing **ascess-1-ai**—an enterprise-grade AI Accessibility Copilot and Web Audit platform powered by Google Gemini AI and Supabase."

---

## 🎬 Act 2: Document Ingestion & Gemini AI Engine (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Screen**: Navigate to `/upload` (Upload Page).
- **Action**: Drag and drop a PDF file or paste a Web URL (`https://...`).
- **Speaker**: "Watch how our Document Processing Engine extracts text, parses page count, cleans OCR artifacts, and generates a structured summary in seconds."
- **Action**: Click "Attach to AI Copilot" and navigate to `/ai`.
- **Speaker**: "Now, our Google Gemini AI Copilot answers questions strictly using the uploaded document context. We can ask it to simplify complex text into Child-Friendly or Senior-Citizen modes."

---

## 🎬 Act 3: Voice Accessibility & Dyslexia Controls (1:30 - 2:15)
- **Screen**: Open the Floating Accessibility Toolbar (`Alt + A`).
- **Action**: Click "Dyslexia Mode" and "Reading Ruler".
- **Speaker**: "For users with dyslexia or cognitive focus needs, one click applies OpenDyslexic letter spacing, extra line height, and a mouse-following reading ruler guide."
- **Action**: Click "Voice Input" and say: *"Go to Dashboard"* or *"Enable Dark Mode"*.
- **Speaker**: "Our voice commands parser allows hands-free navigation across the entire application using natural speech!"

---

## 🎬 Act 4: WCAG Audit Engine & Multi-Format Exports (2:15 - 3:00)
- **Screen**: Navigate to `/reports` (Audit Reports Page).
- **Action**: Enter a URL, run audit, filter by "Critical Priority", and click "Export Options -> Export as Markdown".
- **Speaker**: "Our Smart Scoring Engine generates a 0-100 WCAG score with 5-tier priority issue breakdown and AI code rewrites. Reports can be exported as Markdown, JSON, or Plain Text."
- **Screen**: Show `/analytics` dashboard with score trend graphs.
- **Speaker**: "Thank you for watching **ascess-1-ai**—building an inclusive web for everyone!"
